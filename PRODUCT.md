# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Tyumen browsing the restaurant's takeaway and delivery menu, primarily on a phone while near the restaurant's cash desk. They need to see what is available, what each dish looks like, what it contains, and compare portions and prices quickly.

## Product Purpose

Show the dishes available for takeaway and delivery so visitors can decide what to order and contact the restaurant.

## Positioning

The menu belongs to Gurman restaurant at 66 Lineynaya Street in Tyumen. A distinctive product position is not established.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly open the page on a phone at the cash desk. The current menu has four sections and 29 items. Orders are placed by phone at 21-75-65.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML and CSS, with no build step or JavaScript dependencies.
- The page lists dish names, portion weights, and prices, and links to a PDF menu.
- The page must remain usable on small phone screens, with readable text and touch targets.
- Every dish needs its own real photo. The item-specific photo set has not yet been added to the repository; do not substitute generic or generated food images.
- Four set descriptions are present in the menu. Ingredient descriptions for the remaining dishes still need verified source content and must not be fabricated.
- Online ordering is not currently available; orders are taken by phone.

## Brand Commitments

The restaurant is named «Гурман» and is located in Tyumen. The delivery page may use its own visual approach rather than follow the banquet homepage.

## Evidence on Hand

- Menu content and prices: `delivery/index.html`.
- Existing restaurant and banquet photography: `assets/`; it is not a set of confirmed item-specific delivery photos.
- PDF menu: `files/menu-delivery.pdf`.

## Product Principles

- Make browsing and choosing quickly possible on a phone.
- Keep dish names, portions, and prices easy to compare.
- Do not invent dish ingredients, photos, or service promises.
- Keep calling the restaurant as the order path until online ordering exists.
